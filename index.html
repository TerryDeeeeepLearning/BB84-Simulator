What BB84 does

Alice and Bob want a shared secret key. Alice encodes each random bit in the polarisation of a photon, choosing at random between the rectilinear basis + (H = 0, V = 1) and the diagonal basis × (D = 0, A = 1). Bob measures each photon in a randomly chosen basis. Afterwards they announce only the bases over a public, authenticated channel and keep the bits where the bases agree. Because non-orthogonal states cannot be copied (no-cloning) or measured without disturbance, any eavesdropper leaves errors behind.

The six steps in the simulator

  1. Prepare — Alice picks a basis and bit for every pulse. A real attenuated laser emits a Poisson number of photons (often zero, sometimes two or more).
  2. Transmit & measure — photons cross the fiber (loss 10−αL/10), the polarisation may drift or depolarise, Eve may interfere, and Bob's detectors click according to the Born rule, their efficiency and their dark counts.
  3. Sift — bases are announced; pulses with no click or different bases are discarded (≈ 50 %).
  4. Estimate — a random sample of sifted bits is revealed to measure the quantum bit error rate (QBER). Above ≈ 11 % no secure key is possible, so the session aborts.
  5. Error correction — the Cascade protocol finds and fixes the remaining errors with binary-search parity checks. Every revealed parity counts as information leaked to Eve.
  6. Privacy amplification — the corrected key is compressed with a random Toeplitz matrix (2-universal hashing) to ℓ bits, removing Eve's partial knowledge.

How the physics is simulated

  • State vectors. Each photon is the qubit cos φ|H⟩ + sin φ|V⟩. Measurement outcomes follow the Born rule P(0) = cos²(φ − β) and the state collapses. The four BB84 states, polarisation drift and Pauli errors all stay on this great circle of the Bloch sphere, so tracking φ is an exact state-vector simulation.
  • Source. Single-photon source, or weak coherent pulses with Poisson photon numbers (mean μ). The Lab adds decoy intensities.
  • Channel. Each photon survives the fiber independently; a fixed polarisation rotation θ gives an error sin²θ; depolarisation applies a random Pauli X, Y or Z.
  • Detectors. Two threshold detectors with efficiency η and dark-count probability per gate. Double clicks are assigned a random bit (the squashing model).
  • Eve. Intercept–resend measures in a random basis and resends what she saw (QBER 25 % on attacked pulses). PNS keeps one photon from every multi-photon pulse, blocks single photons and uses a lossless line so Bob's count rate looks normal — she adds no errors. Knows Bob's pattern shows why Bob's bases must be truly random: if they are predictable, Eve measures in Bob's basis and is invisible.
  • Eve's information after hashing is computed exactly over GF(2): the rank of the final-key hash rows relative to the bits she knows plus every parity revealed by Cascade.

Security numbers used

  • Asymptotic key length ℓ = n[1 − h(Q)] − leakEC (Shor–Preskill), threshold Q ≈ 11.0 %.
  • Finite-key length ℓ = n[1 − h(Q + μ)] − leakEC − log₂(2/εsec²εcor), after Tomamichel et al. 2012, with εsec = 10⁻¹⁰ and εcor = 10⁻¹².
  • Decoy-state bounds (vacuum + weak decoy) from Ma, Qi, Zhao & Lo 2005; GLLP for the no-decoy case.

Simplifications to state honestly

  • The classical channel is assumed authenticated; authentication costs are not deducted.
  • Phase error is estimated from the overall sampled QBER (symmetric BB84).
  • With a laser source the Simulator tab still applies the single-photon formula, which Shor–Preskill does not cover — this is what makes the PNS attack succeed there. The Lab shows the decoy-state fix.
  • Detector side-channel attacks (blinding, time-shift) are out of scope; they are what measurement-device-independent QKD addresses.
  • Coherent attacks are covered by the security bounds, not simulated photon by photon.

BB84 在做什麼

Alice 和 Bob 想要一把只有兩人知道的祕密金鑰。Alice 把每個隨機位元編碼在一顆光子的偏振上，並隨機選擇直角基底 +（H = 0、V = 1）或對角基底 ×（D = 0、A = 1）。Bob 用自己隨機選的基底量測每顆光子。之後兩人只在公開但經過認證的通道上公布基底，保留基底相同的位元。因為非正交的量子態無法被複製（不可複製定理），也無法在不擾動的情況下被量測，任何竊聽者都會留下錯誤。

模擬器的六個步驟

  1. 準備：Alice 為每個脈衝選基底和位元。真實的衰減雷射發出的光子數服從 Poisson 分佈（常常是 0 顆，有時 2 顆以上）。
  2. 傳送與量測：光子穿過光纖（損耗 10−αL/10），偏振可能漂移或去極化，Eve 可能介入；Bob 的偵測器依照 Born rule、偵測效率和暗計數決定是否響、響哪一個。
  3. 篩選：公布基底，丟掉沒有偵測到或基底不同的脈衝（約 50%）。
  4. 估計：公開一部分隨機抽出的位元，量測量子位元錯誤率（QBER）。超過約 11% 就不可能有安全金鑰，本輪中止。
  5. 糾錯：Cascade 協定用二分搜尋的同位檢查找出並修正剩下的錯誤。每個公開的同位位元都算是洩漏給 Eve 的資訊。
  6. 隱私放大：用隨機 Toeplitz 矩陣（2-universal 雜湊）把修正後的金鑰壓縮成 ℓ 位元，消除 Eve 掌握的部分資訊。

物理如何被模擬

  • 狀態向量。每顆光子是 qubit cos φ|H⟩ + sin φ|V⟩。量測結果依照 Born rule P(0) = cos²(φ − β)，量完後態會塌縮。BB84 的四個態、偏振漂移和 Pauli 錯誤都落在 Bloch 球的同一個大圓上，所以追蹤角度 φ 就是精確的狀態向量模擬。
  • 光源。單光子源，或光子數服從 Poisson 分佈（平均 μ）的弱相干光脈衝。實驗室分頁另外加入誘騙態強度。
  • 通道。每顆光子獨立決定能否穿過光纖；固定的偏振旋轉 θ 造成 sin²θ 的錯誤率；去極化會隨機施加 Pauli X、Y 或 Z。
  • 偵測器。兩個臨界偵測器，有偵測效率 η 和每閘暗計數機率。兩個同時響（雙響）時隨機指定一個位元（squashing 模型）。
  • Eve。攔截重送：用隨機基底量測再重送她看到的結果（受攻擊脈衝的 QBER 為 25%）。PNS：從每個多光子脈衝留下一顆光子，擋下單光子脈衝，並用無損線路讓 Bob 的計數率看起來正常，不會造成錯誤。已知 Bob 的基底圖樣：說明 Bob 的基底為什麼必須真正隨機；只要可以預測，Eve 用 Bob 的基底量測就完全隱形。
  • 雜湊後 Eve 的資訊量是在 GF(2) 上精確計算的：最終金鑰雜湊矩陣相對於「Eve 確切知道的位元＋Cascade 公開的所有同位」的秩。

使用的安全性公式

  • 漸近金鑰長度 ℓ = n[1 − h(Q)] − leakEC（Shor–Preskill），門檻 Q ≈ 11.0%。
  • 有限金鑰長度 ℓ = n[1 − h(Q + μ)] − leakEC − log₂(2/εsec²εcor)，依 Tomamichel 等人 2012，εsec = 10⁻¹⁰、εcor = 10⁻¹²。
  • 誘騙態界限（真空＋弱誘騙態）採用 Ma、Qi、Zhao、Lo 2005；無誘騙態時採用 GLLP。

誠實說明的簡化

  • 假設古典通道已經過認證，沒有扣除認證的成本。
  • 相位錯誤用整體抽樣的 QBER 估計（對稱 BB84）。
  • 模擬器分頁選雷射光源時，仍然套用單光子公式，而 Shor–Preskill 的證明不涵蓋這種情況；這正是 PNS 攻擊在那裡能成功的原因。實驗室分頁展示了誘騙態的解法。
  • 偵測器旁通道攻擊（致盲、時間位移）不在模擬範圍內，那是量測裝置無關 QKD（MDI-QKD）要解決的問題。
  • 相干攻擊由安全性界限涵蓋，沒有逐顆光子模擬。
